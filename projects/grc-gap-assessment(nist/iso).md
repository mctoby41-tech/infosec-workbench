# GRC Gap Assessment

## Objective
Assess a fake company against NIST CSF or ISO 27001 and produce a gap report + risk register.

## Framework
* NIST CSF
* ISO 27001

## Tasks
1. Evaluate current controls.
2. Identify gaps.
3. Build a risk register.
4. Provide remediation recommendations.

## Gap Report
Summaries of missing or weak controls.
